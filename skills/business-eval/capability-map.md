# Reference capability map

10 L1 domains, 56 L2 capabilities. Codes are stable: never renumber them, even when the wording is adapted to an industry. That keeps evaluations comparable across companies and across time.

Each L2 row has:

- **Scope**: what the capability covers, written so it applies to any business.
- **Signals**: where evidence usually shows up (interview, public, document).

## 01 Manage strategy, performance and transformation

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01.1 | Cascade the strategy | Turn strategy into goals, targets and initiatives that each unit and team owns. | OKRs or BSC, planning calendar, how a frontline team knows its targets. |
| 01.2 | Manage the operating model and organizational transformation | Design how the company is organized to deliver value (structure, governance, decision rights) and change it deliberately. | Org chart history, reorg cadence, RACI, leadership statements on restructuring. |
| 01.3 | Prioritize and run the transformation portfolio | Select, fund, sequence and stop change initiatives as one portfolio. | PMO, stage gates, killed projects, initiative count vs. capacity. |
| 01.4 | Manage economic performance, SG&A and benefit realization | Track unit economics and overhead, and prove that initiatives delivered the promised benefits. | Margin trend, SG&A as % of revenue, post-investment reviews. |
| 01.5 | Manage enterprise risk and business continuity | Identify, own and mitigate strategic and operational risks; keep operating through disruption. | Risk register, BCP/DR tests, incident history, risk factors in filings. |
| 01.6 | Experiment with and scale business innovations | Run small bets on new models, products or markets, and scale the ones that work. | Pilots, venture arms, new revenue lines, time from pilot to scale. |

## 02 Structure capital and manage financial resources

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 02.1 | Structure funding and capital partnerships | Raise and structure equity, debt and partner capital at the right cost and terms. | Cap table, debt terms, funding rounds, covenants, investor quality. |
| 02.2 | Allocate capital and evaluate investments | Decide where capital goes with a consistent method (hurdle rates, business cases, post-mortems). | Capex process, IRR/payback rules, M&A track record. |
| 02.3 | Manage credit, collateral and customer financing | Decide who gets credit and on what terms; secure exposures; offer financing that helps sell (trade credit, leasing, in-kind or structured deals). | Credit policy, bad-debt rate, financing products, collateral practices. |
| 02.4 | Manage liquidity and financial risk | Keep cash available and hedge currency, interest rate and commodity exposure. | Cash runway, cash-flow forecast accuracy, hedging policy, FX exposure. |
| 02.5 | Manage receivables and collections | Turn invoices into cash on time. | DSO, aging buckets, collection process, write-offs. |
| 02.6 | Manage financial results and obligations | Close the books, report, pay taxes and meet financial obligations accurately and on time. | Close duration, audit opinions, restatements, tax disputes. |

## 03 Manage market and commercial intelligence

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 03.1 | Analyze markets, buyer contexts and demand cycles | Understand market size, structure, cultures of the customer base and the cycles (seasonal, budget, macro) that drive demand. | Market sizing, seasonality models, research subscriptions. |
| 03.2 | Segment customers and prioritize high-value accounts | Group customers by value and need, and focus effort on the ones that matter most. | Segmentation model, account tiers, revenue concentration. |
| 03.3 | Manage competitive intelligence and white-space opportunities | Track competitors and find openings (expiring protections, unserved niches, competitor exits). | Competitor tracking, win/loss analysis, launches into gaps. |
| 03.4 | Manage value propositions by customer segment | Define and test a distinct value proposition for each target segment. | Segment-specific messaging, pricing and packaging; win rates per segment. |

## 04 Manage innovation and portfolio differentiation

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 04.1 | Manage a differentiated portfolio | Shape the product/service portfolio so it is meaningfully different from competitors, not only cheaper. | Share of revenue from differentiated lines, price premium, portfolio reviews. |
| 04.2 | Develop global partnerships and licensing | Bring in or license out technology, brands or products through partners. | Licensing deals, distribution agreements, alliance announcements. |
| 04.3 | Develop offerings with differentiated performance | Engineer products or services that measurably outperform alternatives. | Benchmarks, third-party tests, technical claims customers repeat. |
| 04.4 | Adapt offerings to local conditions | Tailor offerings to the specific conditions of the markets served (climate, regulation, culture, infrastructure). | Local variants, local R&D, localization depth. |
| 04.5 | Manage product development | Run the pipeline from idea to launch with gates, budgets and timelines. | Pipeline size, time to market, stage-gate process, launch hit rate. |
| 04.6 | Manage product lifecycle and intellectual property | Manage products from launch to retirement, and protect and use IP (patents, trademarks, trade secrets, data). | Patent portfolio, sunset decisions, IP disputes, SKU rationalization. |

## 05 Manage regulatory affairs

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 05.1 | Manage regulatory strategy and intelligence | Anticipate regulatory change and shape a strategy around it. | Regulatory roadmap, trade-association roles, comment letters. |
| 05.2 | Manage regulatory studies and dossiers | Produce the studies, evidence and documentation that regulators require. | Dossier backlog, study timelines, approval cycle times. |
| 05.3 | Obtain and maintain registrations, traceability and authorizations | Get and keep the licenses, registrations, certifications and traceability that allow the company to operate and sell. | License inventory, certification list, lapses, traceability systems. |

## 06 Manage production and operational efficiency

"Production" means whatever the company delivers: manufacturing for goods, delivery operations for services, platform operations for software.

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06.1 | Create and scale production solutions | Design production or delivery processes and scale them up with demand. | Scale-up history, capacity utilization, new sites or lines. |
| 06.2 | Manage contract production and outsourced services | Produce for others or have others produce for you (tolling, CDMO, white label, outsourced delivery). | Contract manufacturing agreements, make-vs-buy mix, partner SLAs. |
| 06.3 | Assure quality of materials and products | Control quality of inputs and outputs so they meet specification. | Defect rates, QMS certifications, complaints, returns. |
| 06.4 | Manage assets and operational reliability | Maintain equipment, facilities or infrastructure so they are available when needed. | Uptime, OEE, maintenance strategy, unplanned downtime. |
| 06.5 | Manage operational safety and environmental performance | Keep people safe and environmental impact controlled during operations. | Incident rates, environmental permits and fines, ESG reports. |

## 07 Manage planning, procurement and logistics

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07.1 | Forecast demand and sales | Predict what will be sold, where and when. | Forecast accuracy, forecast process, bias. |
| 07.2 | Run integrated planning (demand, supply, production) | Balance demand, supply and capacity in one plan (S&OP / IBP). | S&OP cadence, who attends, plan adherence. |
| 07.3 | Develop and qualify supply sources | Find, qualify and develop suppliers, including alternatives for critical inputs. | Approved supplier list, single-source exposure, qualification time. |
| 07.4 | Negotiate and manage purchasing and suppliers | Buy at the right cost and terms and manage supplier performance. | Spend under management, supplier scorecards, savings. |
| 07.5 | Manage foreign trade and imports | Handle cross-border buying and selling: customs, duties, trade compliance. | Import share of COGS, customs incidents, trade-compliance function. |
| 07.6 | Manage inventory, allocation and availability | Hold the right stock in the right place and allocate it when supply is short. | Inventory turns, stockouts, obsolescence write-offs. |
| 07.7 | Distribute products and track deliveries | Move products to customers and give visibility of delivery status. | OTIF, logistics cost per unit, delivery tracking. |

## 08 Manage market access and commercial effectiveness

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 08.7 | Manage commercial effectiveness and productivity | Measure and improve the yield of the sales and marketing organization. | Revenue per rep, CAC, quota attainment, coverage. |

## 09 Manage customer experience and performance in use

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 09.1 | Manage relationships, digital channels and customer experience | Manage the end-to-end relationship, including digital touchpoints. | NPS/CSAT, app or portal adoption, review sites. |

## 10 Develop people, technology and a scalable organization

| Code | Capability | Scope | Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10.1 | Develop people, culture and competencies | Hire, develop and keep the people and culture the strategy needs. | Turnover, Glassdoor-type reviews, training spend, succession plans. |
| 10.2 | Manage knowledge | Capture and reuse what the organization knows. | Documentation, knowledge bases, dependence on key individuals. |

| 10.6 | Manage legal affairs and compliance | Handle contracts, litigation, and compliance with laws and internal policies. | Litigation load, compliance program, sanctions/anti-bribery controls. |
| 10.7 | Provide corporate shared services | Run back-office services (HR ops, finance ops, facilities, procurement of indirects) efficiently. | Shared-service center, back-office cost per employee, SLAs. |
